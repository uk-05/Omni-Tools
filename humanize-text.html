How AI Detectors Work

Detectors like Turnitin, GPTZero, and CopyLeaks analyze two primary linguistic signals:

  • Perplexity: A measure of word predictability. AI uses the most mathematically likely next word.
  • Burstiness: Variation in sentence length and structure. AI writes in steady, monotonous 18-24 word blocks, whereas humans mix short punchy fragments with long thoughts.
